# Quickstart (For Hydrologists & Disaster Management)

This page walks through downloading, installing, and running OpenFloodAI as
a ready-made program, with no coding involved. If you are a developer
setting up the project from source instead, see the
[Developer Quickstart](dev-quickstart.md).

## 1. Download the app

Go to the
[OpenFloodAI Releases page](https://github.com/lamadipen/OpenFloodAI/releases/latest)
and download the file for your computer:

- **Windows** — download `OpenFloodAI.exe`.
- **Mac** — download `OpenFloodAI-macOS.zip`, then double-click it to unzip
  it into `OpenFloodAI.app`.

## 2. Open it for the first time

The app is not yet digitally signed, so your computer will show a warning
the first time you open it. This is expected — it happens because the app
was not purchased through an app store, not because anything is wrong.

**On a Mac:** right-click (or Control-click) `OpenFloodAI.app` and choose
**Open**. A warning will appear saying Apple could not verify the app; click
**Open** again to confirm. You only need to do this once — after that, it
opens normally.

**On Windows:** double-click `OpenFloodAI.exe`. If you see a blue "Windows
protected your PC" screen, click **More info**, then click **Run anyway**.

## 3. What happens when it opens

A small OpenFloodAI icon appears in your system tray (Windows, bottom-right
of the screen) or menu bar (Mac, top-right of the screen), and your web
browser opens automatically to the app. That browser window is where you'll
do all your work — creating sites, reviewing videos, and reading reports.

## 4. Where your files are saved

Everything you create — camera settings, videos you add, notes, and reports
— is saved on your own computer, in a private folder that only OpenFloodAI
uses. You don't need to remember where that is: click the tray/menu-bar icon
and choose **Open Sites Folder** any time you want to see your files
directly.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. It all stays on the computer you're using.

## 5. Closing the app

Click the tray/menu-bar icon and choose **Quit**. Simply closing the browser
tab does not stop the app — use **Quit** to fully close it.

## Where to go next

Once the app is open in your browser, follow
[Your First End-to-End Review](learning/end-to-end-workflow.md) — its first
step will already be checked off, since the app is running.

## A safety reminder

OpenFloodAI's reports mean "please review this evidence," not "there is a
confirmed flood." It is a review tool to support your team's own judgment,
not an automatic public warning system.
