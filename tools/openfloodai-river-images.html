Download River Images and Video

Save images for a chosen hour, or download a public USGS camera's latest time-lapse video.

This tool uses the internet only when you choose a download. It saves media on this computer. It does not upload your files, add videos to a site, run validation, or send warnings.

Use a camera page from apps.usgs.gov/hivis/camera/.

Download the latest available MP4 for the camera above. It uses recent camera images, not the date or hour below. Availability varies by camera; the exact capture period is not confirmed.

Images for a selected hour

Cameo defaults to America/Denver. For another camera, enter its IANA timezone if automatic lookup is unavailable. Your computer's timezone is not used.

We look for the first image in each 15-minute window: :00, :15, :30 and :45. The actual capture time may be later than the start of that window. Missing images are reported, not replaced with images from another hour.

Choose a date and hour to start.
